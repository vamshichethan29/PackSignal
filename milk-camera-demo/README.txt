PackSignal fictional milk-carton camera-intake test

Files
- fictional-milk-carton-photo.jpg: one front-and-right-side photo-style image.
- fictional-milk-carton-turn.mp4: five-second silent turn showing front, side, and back views.
- product-specification.json: fictional source document to add as Product specification.

Try it
1. Open PackSignal and start a new artwork review.
2. Add product-specification.json as the product specification.
3. In Camera & Photo Intake, choose the JPG with Choose photos, or choose the MP4 with Choose video.
4. Complete the review, then inspect the 2D artwork pages. Confirm the photographed face mapping in Sources before opening 3D context.

The sample intentionally conflicts on net quantity: the artwork says 1 L while the specification says 946 mL. The files are synthetic and contain no real product or brand. Photo/video intake extracts representative stills; it does not infer precise package dimensions or CAD folds. A reviewer must confirm face mapping, and camera-only 3D remains illustrative.
