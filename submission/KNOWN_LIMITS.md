# Known limits and remaining validation

- One live Gemini 3.8 Flash review completed on the fictional Fieldday V1 files with one outbound attempt. This single integration run is not independent accuracy evaluation; model/project access and future quota remain account-specific.
- Gemini is the only implemented LLM provider. Up to five server-side credentials can be configured through ignored `.env.local` variables; the UI is read-only and never receives them. Local extraction and deterministic checks finish before Gemini runs in the background. A quota or temporary-provider failure can put one credential into cooldown and trigger bounded failover to another configured credential. The default session ceiling counts all outbound attempts, including retries and failover, and resets on server restart; it is not a persistent billing cap and does not create extra provider quota.
- When AI fails or is deferred, the review explicitly continues with deterministic local checks. Offline tests cover bounded retries on one credential, cooldown behavior, and the transport behavior used by another credential; they do not simulate the server's full coordinator against Google's quota system. A live end-to-end quota/failover exercise remains outstanding, and deterministic continuation does not validate visual checks for image-only artwork.
- Local development binds to loopback. A Render blueprint is included but this source snapshot has not been deployed. Hosting requires the configured access password, exact public origin, persistent storage and a paid service plan; do not expose the upload app without that access gate.
- Local checks support a defined set of English document labels, copy comparisons, recognized claims, unit conversions and nutrition patterns. Arbitrary layout, multilingual meaning and unseen product schemas may remain unverified.
- Claim recognition uses common phrases and entries in the supplied JSON register; it is not exhaustive claim detection. SKU-scoped entries must match a unique JSON specification SKU, and conditional entries remain under human review. A match covers recognized entries only.
- AI vision sends artwork pages first, including searchable PDFs, in batches of three and up to 12 pages (at most four successful image requests). Additional pages are explicitly listed as omitted. A quota, cooldown, session ceiling, malformed response, or incomplete transcript can stop later batches; partial AI results are retained, surfaced, and not cached as complete. Pages without a usable visual transcript stay unverified. A transcription cannot prove absence and still needs visual confirmation.
- Native PDF text may include invisible, overlapping, outdated or non-consumer printing text. A detected string is not proof of visible printed content. The reviewer must compare it with the image.
- PDF coordinate highlights cover extracted text lines and can be approximate. Rotated-page coordinates are omitted rather than fabricated.
- Global copy matching does not verify bleed, color separations, barcode quality, ingredient completeness or full regulatory compliance. A separate nutrition plausibility screen checks a few arithmetic relationships; it is not nutrition science validation. Optional panel-zone checks use reviewer-drawn rectangles and extracted text boxes. Typography screens estimate PDF text bounding-box height at the supplied page scale; they do not measure actual glyph height or validate an applicable legal minimum. Allergen cues cover explicit `CONTAINS` and uppercase text only; bold font metadata is not analyzed.
- Numeric comparisons require a clear source value, unit and serving basis. Multiple nutrition panels/ambiguous source columns stay under review.
- User confirmation records a human assertion; PackSignal does not independently authenticate the approver or validate their authority.
- Files are bounded, but this is not a hardened hostile-file sandbox. Do not open the server to public uploads.
- Exported review snapshots use a local Ed25519 key. Downloadable Prepress Report packages (`.zip`) contain `report.pdf`, `review.json`, `audit-trail.json`, `verification-instructions.txt`, `report.html`, bounded defect crops in `evidence/`, and `verification-manifest.json`. The manifest is signed with the local Ed25519 private key and protects SHA-256 hashes of all package files and the chained audit trail digest. Offline verification via `node scripts/verify-report.mjs report.zip` confirms byte-level cryptographic integrity against the manifest; it does NOT establish the real-world identity or regulatory authority of the signer without an independent PKI certificate. The PDF bytes themselves are protected by the signed manifest, not an embedded Adobe digital certificate. Evidence crops are extracted losslessly from locally rendered artwork rasterizations with contextual padding; they do not alter original artwork or fabricate replacement visuals. If an artwork coordinate box is absent, unlocated findings are retained with textual proof rather than a fabricated visual crop. Final legal compliance, technical press readiness, and print release require authorized human approval. The private key is stored in the local data directory, so this does not authenticate an approver or protect against an administrator who can replace both data and key. Audit events remain local files; concurrent edits from multiple users are not supported.
- Fictional fixtures are known development examples, not independent evaluation data. Their expected results never enter the inference path.
- The zero-dependency Node regression suite runs with `npm test`, including mocked provider retry/quota and multi-batch image coverage tests. `npm run evaluate` checks 21 hand-authored synthetic deterministic-engine cases, including clean cases, defects, ambiguity, extraction gaps and a revision regression. These developer fixtures are deterministic regression tests, NOT independent validation.
- An independent mini-evaluation framework is implemented in `evaluation/independent/` and executed via `npm run evaluate:blind` (`node scripts/score-blind-evaluation.mjs`). This pilot evaluates 3 previously unseen packaging sets (`CASE-01` Mismatch, `CASE-02` Clean, `CASE-03` Ambiguous) with 12 unit checks. This pilot evaluated three selected packaging sets. Results describe performance strictly on the included supported check instances and are not representative of all packaging artwork or regulatory requirements. Because the dataset is small (12 units), metrics must be reported as pilot observations (e.g. Precision 1/3, Recall 1/3) with explicit numerators and denominators, and undefined denominators display N/A. Under no circumstances should marketing claims such as "98% accuracy", "FDA certified", or "outperforms competitor" be made based on this pilot sample. Full independent human reviewer sign-off remains formally pending. Smoke tests via `npm run smoke` exercise local HTTP workflows, report export, and Ed25519 seal verification in an isolated temporary store. Live provider outage/quota recovery, broad external packaging reviewer feedback, and production load/security review remain outstanding.
- The artwork map visualizes only findings that have an extracted artwork box. It is check coverage, not calibrated model confidence, overall risk, or proof that uncolored regions were examined.
- Visual reference labels in a discrepancy view are copied from supplied source material. They do not edit the artwork or constitute an approved replacement.
- Sibling-SKU screening runs only when multiple structured SKU specifications include variant values and the reviewer has resolved the variant. A matching sibling name is a review prompt; intentional cross-promotion can produce a false alarm. The check does not infer variants from unstructured prose.
- Spreadsheet product-family screening requires clearly labeled table headers. It matches recognizable sibling-row identifiers, ingredients, allergen statements, quantities and barcode digits against extracted artwork text. It can miss unusual schemas or partially extracted strings and can flag intentional cross-promotion; each finding needs a person to verify the product row and artwork.
- Environmental wording detection uses a bounded list of common phrases. A claims-register entry or reference string is only a lead for the reviewer to inspect; PackSignal does not open or authenticate a certification, test substantiation, determine claim truth, or decide whether a claim is lawful.
- Cross-language parity only compares exact, required phrase pairs explicitly supplied in a structured `translation_pairs` field. A missing phrase remains *Unable to verify* until source authority and artwork coverage are confirmed. Presence of all phrases does not prove that a translation preserves meaning, safety instructions, or legal effect.
- ASCII DXF dielines preserve supported `LINE` / `LWPOLYLINE` coordinates and `TEXT` / `MTEXT` labels. Explicit layer or linetype names can classify strokes as cut, crease/fold/score, glue, or perforation; color is never treated as semantic evidence. A unique face label inside a closed panel polyline can suggest that panel's crop. Blocks, splines, arcs, hatches, dimensions, paper-space geometry, arbitrary CAD extensions, and binary DXF are not parsed; inspect parser warnings and the original source. PDF/image dielines still provide only text-label and unclassified visual-line cues. For every format, face mapping remains reviewer-confirmed, and carton, pouch, bottle-label, and sleeve 3D forms remain illustrative rather than a CAD solid or fold simulation.
- Designer change tickets are intentionally evidence-bound. They do not guess font family, minimum point size, software layer, exact position or a replacement phrase unless that information is present in the supplied sources.
- Market selections are limited starter review prompts linked to official sources. They do not implement all jurisdiction, product, language, typography, allergen-formatting, or category-specific requirements.
- Language detection is heuristic and only raises review prompts after multiple cues. It does not verify translation accuracy, scripts beyond the supported cues, or legally required languages.
- Barcode and QR recognition records only code type and approximate location. It does not validate payload assignment, safety, print grade, quiet zones, retail readiness, or whether a code is required. No decoder output is *Unable to verify*, not proof of absence.
- Deep verification and packaging discrepancy investigation boundaries:
  - The organizer content sheet contains "Draft / In Review" formulation data (cell E3). Discrepancies between the carton and spreadsheet are strictly classified as `SOURCE_ARTWORK_MISMATCH` or `REVIEW`; PackSignal does not convert draft source conflicts into definitive regulatory violations without brand owner resolution.
  - Atwater 4-4-9 calorie screening (e.g. 535 kcal computed vs 166 kcal printed) is an arithmetic screening plausibility check, not a certified FDA audit. Food-specific factors (e.g. USDA Handbook 74), digestible carbohydrate formulations, or dietary fiber deductions may lawfully alter final label calories.
  - Daily Value calculations apply US FDA 2016 Reference Daily Values (21 CFR 101.9) for adults and children >= 4 years. Specialized infant, toddler, or medical food DRVs are not evaluated.
  - PDF drawing sequence occlusion analysis detects text spans (such as the legacy EU table "Typical Values per 100g" at seqno 227) that are covered by subsequent opaque vector fills (seqno 241). These are classified as `POTENTIALLY_OCCLUDED` and excluded from consumer-facing copy checks; physical separation plate verification is still recommended prior to cylinder engraving or platemaking.
  - Dieline construction text (e.g. "Art orientation", "Copy free", "gl1") on technical CAD layers is classified as `PRODUCTION_GUIDE` and excluded from label copy; it does not satisfy statutory consumer declarations.
  - Major food allergen disclosure checks recognize that FALCPA (21 U.S.C. 343(w)) permits allergen disclosure directly within the ingredient list using common or usual names; an absent "Contains:" statement is therefore flagged as a company policy review rather than an automatic federal violation.
  - Company rulebook DF-QA-LBL-003 is tracked as an authored third-party test fixture. In-force vs proposed lifecycle gating is strictly enforced (Rule DF-21 remains `PROPOSED` and produces no findings).
- Multi-Jurisdiction Regulatory Switcher & Cross-Market Review Boundaries:
  - Supported Jurisdictions:
    - USA: US Food and Drug Administration (FDA) requirements under 21 CFR Part 101 (21 CFR 101.9 Nutrition Facts panel, 101.9(c)(1) Calories prominence, 101.9(b) Serving size declarations, 101.9(c)(6)(iii) Added sugars, 101.9(c)(9) nutrient-specific Daily Values, 21 CFR 101.4 ingredients, FALCPA/FASTER Act 9 major food allergens including sesame, 21 CFR 101.105 dual-unit net quantity, and 21 CFR 101.5 manufacturer/distributor identity).
    - European Union: Regulation (EU) No 1169/2011 on Food Information to Consumers (FIC) (Article 32(2) mandatory per 100 g or per 100 ml basis, Article 30(1) & 32(1) mandatory dual energy declaration in both kJ and kcal with 1 kcal = 4.184 kJ ratio verification, Annex XV standard 7 mandatory nutrients, Article 30(1) mandatory Salt declaration where Salt = Sodium × 2.5, Article 21(1)(b) allergen typesetting emphasis, Article 13(2) x-height legibility thresholds of >= 1.2 mm or >= 0.9 mm for packages < 80 cm², and Article 9 mandatory particulars).
    - India: Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) under the Food Safety and Standards (Labelling and Display) Regulations, 2020 as amended (Regulation 5(1)(d) 14-digit FSSAI license number and logo, Regulation 5(2) statutory green circle in square (Vegetarian) and brown triangle in square (Non-Vegetarian) symbols, Regulation 5(3) nutritional information per 100g/ml and per serve with %RDA, Regulation 5(4) allergen declaration schedule, Regulation 5(7) manufacturing and best before / expiry date marking, and Regulation 5(10) metric net quantity).
  - Effective Date & Version Handling:
    - Statutory commencement dates are strictly gated against the evaluation effective date. Future statutory amendments (such as FSSAI Labelling and Display First Amendment Regulations, 2026, commencing July 1, 2027 for front-of-pack nutrition labeling HFSS thresholds) are classified as `future_requirement` (informational preview) as of October 2026, and are NEVER treated as an active violation or legal defect prior to statutory commencement.
  - Computer Vision Regulatory Symbol Screening:
    - Symbol detection (`lib/regulatory-symbols.py`) performs raster color segmentation and geometric contour analysis (aspect ratio 0.75-1.33, fill ratio 18%-75%) for green circle-in-square and brown triangle-in-square symbols. Unclassified symbols, blurry artwork crops, or missing image rasters return `review` or `unable`, never a false definitive absence claim.
  - Review Modes:
    - "Target Market Review" verifies compliance against designated destination market rules.
    - "Cross-Market What-If Preview" evaluates differences against another market without labeling the package defective solely because it was designed for its home market.
  - Non-Legal Certification Safeguard:
    - PackSignal's multi-jurisdiction regulatory checks are deterministic packaging engineering and prepress verification aids. They do NOT constitute statutory legal advice, regulatory authorization, or an official FDA, EU EUR-Lex, FSSAI, or Esko compliance certificate. Commercial food labeling releases require qualified regulatory and legal counsel review.
- Multi-Format 3D Packaging Studio Boundaries:
  - Folding Carton: Forward kinematics animation rotates child panels from a 0% flat dieline to a 100% folded 3D box based on validated DXF crease lines and hinge connectivity. Incomplete dielines or missing crease tags gracefully fall back to an illustrative 6-face preview clearly labeled as such.
  - Beverage Can: Generates a cylindrical 3D model with brushed aluminum chimes, lid, pull tab, and bottom dome. Flat label artwork maps horizontally around the cylinder with seam angle alignment. When physical label width and can circumference disagree, a discrepancy warning is presented rather than silently stretching the label. Does not claim manufacturing-grade metal-drawing or bead-profile deformation simulation.
  - Bottle: Constructs a rotationally symmetric lathed body profile (glass or plastic), shoulder, neck, cap, and wrap-label cylinder mesh. Labels are projected onto the cylindrical body; complex shoulder tapers or double-curved glass geometries are not warped without explicit paramaterized surface mapping.
  - Stand-up Flexible Pouch: Custom buffer geometry models front/back pillow curvature, top and side heat-seal flanges, and an expanding bottom gusset. The inflation animation (0% flat to 100% expanded) is an illustrative volume preview; it does not compute finite-element thin-film wrinkle propagation, tension distribution, or barrier rupture physics. Unmapped rear areas display an explicit placeholder rather than duplicating front artwork.
  - Premium Finishes & Substrates: MeshPhysicalMaterial approximates PBR substrate properties (matte paperboard, gloss-coated, white plastic, clear PET with transmission, and aluminum) and selective finishes (Spot UV gloss, gold foil, silver foil, embossing preview) inside source-supplied or reviewer-defined region masks. These are visual approximations; press ink-oil repellency, die stamping pressure, and anilox transfer tolerances remain production-specific.
  - 3D Defect Pins: Evidence markers are projected strictly from verified 2D artwork bounding boxes and transform rigidly with the geometry. Findings without located artwork coordinates do not receive fabricated 3D pins.
- Wave D Advanced Deterministic Rule Compiler & Press Quality Radar Boundaries:
  - Deterministic Rule Compiler & AST Schema:
    - Rule compilation strictly distinguishes between binding mandates and non-binding proposed amendments (`PROPOSED_NONBINDING` Gate 1) or permissive/voluntary statements (`EXEMPTION_OR_VOLUNTARY` Gate 2).
    - Unverified rule sources or unapproved specifications require reviewer confirmation and do not fail artwork.
    - Supported packaging rule families evaluate strictly against extracted facts, approved specifications, and parsed rule parameters. When required facts are missing, the engine yields `UNABLE_TO_VERIFY` or `REVIEW_REQUIRED` rather than fabricating values or assuming silent compliance.
  - Six Packaging Rule Families:
    - `BARCODE_GTIN_MATCH`: Validates GS1 Mod-10 check digits and verifies 14-digit zero-padded canonical GTIN equality. Does NOT evaluate optical or laser print quality grades (ISO/IEC 15416) from raster screenshots or decoded barcode text alone.
    - `STATUTORY_SYMBOL_PRESENCE`: Enforces mandatory statutory marks (e.g., FSSAI vegetarian/non-vegetarian symbols). Distinguishes statutory symbols from voluntary certification claims (e.g. Certified Organic, Certified Vegan); unverified voluntary claims prompt for certificate documentation (`VOLUNTARY_CERTIFICATE_UNVERIFIED`) rather than triggering statutory misbranding violations.
    - `MULTILINGUAL_PAIR_REQUIREMENT`: Enforces parallel language declarations based on market-specific requirements (e.g., Canada English/French). Does not impose universal French requirements on EU or US packages, and respects statutory exemptions (e.g. registered trademarks, local test markets).
    - `MANUFACTURER_ADDRESS_DECLARATION`: Validates US 21 CFR 101.5 business name and address components. Recognizes 21 CFR 101.5(d) telephone directory exception where street address is legally omissible if listed in a current directory.
    - `STORAGE_INSTRUCTION_MANDATE`: Checks mandatory storage condition statements (dry, ambient, refrigerated, frozen) and flags physical thermal regime contradictions (e.g. "Keep Refrigerated" on frozen inventory).
    - `NUTRIENT_CONTENT_CLAIM_LIMIT`: Validates US Low Sodium claims under 21 CFR 101.61 (<= 140 mg sodium per RACC, with 50g small-RACC basis and 100g meal-basis criteria). Requires explicit quantitative nutrition facts; missing sodium facts yield `UNABLE_TO_VERIFY`.
  - Press Quality Radar & Print Physics:
    - Total Area Coverage (TAC / Total Ink Limit): Calculates $C + M + Y + K$ against press-specific substrate profiles (e.g. Flexo Film 280%, Sheetfed Offset 300%, Gravure Metal 340%, Digital Folding Carton 320%). Never enforces a universal 300% limit.
    - CMYK Separations: Analyzes native `DeviceCMYK` separation objects. Unmanaged RGB artwork returns `UNSUPPORTED_COLOR_SPACE` / warning rather than fabricating fake CMYK plate percentages. Spot colors are inventoried as independent spot separations and not converted into synthetic process CMYK plates.
    - Barcode Print Quality vs Identifier Verification: An ISO/IEC 15416 or 15415 grade (e.g., Grade 3.8/A) is ONLY reported when hardware-calibrated verifier inspection evidence is supplied. When uncalibrated screen pixels or simple barcode decoders are used, the engine truthfully reports `VERIFIER_EVIDENCE_MISSING` and provides screening dimensions without pretending to have an ISO grade.
    - Bleed, Trim & Crease Clearances: Geometric preflight screens evaluate vector bounding coordinates against structural cut and crease lines. Flap margins and non-consumer gluing areas are classified as production guides and excluded from consumer copy checks.
